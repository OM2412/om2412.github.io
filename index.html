The engineering decision

AI suggestions remain separate from rule-based verdicts. A candidate's days and unit must match its source quote before it is displayed as a payment candidate.

Handling messy inputs

Text-based documents use embedded text; supported scanned pages use local Tesseract OCR with explicit page and upload limits.

A focused payment-term prototype, not legal advice or comprehensive legal review. Inspect the original documents and OCR text.
